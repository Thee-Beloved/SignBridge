Multimodal Pipeline

4 Meta AI Models. One Unified Bridge.

Every step operates in synchronized harmony — converting raw spoken video into high-accuracy, culturally authentic Kenyan Sign Language.

STEP 01
🎙️

Muse Wonder 2

muse-wonder-1.0

Extracts dialogue through background music and noisy street ambience. Understands Sheng (Swahili-English slang) and generates word-level timestamps.

Target: Noisy social media audio
STEP 02
🧠

Muse Spark 1.3

muse-spark-1.3

Reconstructs English grammar into KSL Gloss (SOV structure). Drops redundant copulas (is/am/are) and maps cultural signs (Matatu, Ugali, Harambee).

"Add two tomatoes" → TOMATO TWO ADD
STEP 03
👁️

SAM 3.1 Vision

sam-3.1

Analyzes video frames to track speaker faces, cooking pans, or product demos. Calculates the optimal safe corner to prevent obscuring critical content.

Priority: Never cover faces or active hands
STEP 04
🎭

Muse Image + FFmpeg

muse-image-1.0

Generates a consistent Kenyan interpreter avatar with clear hand gestures. Deduplicates frames and renders an accessible composite MP4 in under 90s.

Avatar Seed: Consistent Kenyan Interpreter
