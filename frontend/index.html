Multimodal Pipeline

Engineered on Meta AI Models

Four models chained in a deterministic, zero-hallucination workflow to produce broadcast-quality signing overlays.

01 muse-wonder-1.0

Audio Transcription

Extracts dialogue through background music and street ambience. Robust to Sheng slang and multi-lingual Kenyan accents with word-level timestamps.

Output: Timed English tokens
02 muse-spark-1.3

Linguistic Grammar

Translates English SVO into KSL SOV gloss notation. Drops copulas, applies Kenyan spatial grammar, and preserves cultural concepts (Ugali, Matatu, Harambee).

Grammar: Subject-Object-Verb (SOV)
03 sam-3.1

Spatial Vision

Analyzes video frames to track speaker faces, cooking utensils, and active gestures. Computes safe corners to ensure the signing avatar never blocks vital visual content.

Safety: Face & hand avoidance lock
04 muse-image-1.0

Avatar Synthesis

Synthesizes a consistent Kenyan interpreter avatar with anatomically clear hand shapes. Employs hardware-accelerated FFmpeg compositing with frame deduplication.

Consistency: Fixed seed interpreter

Human & Social Impact

Why Sign Bridge Matters

Eliminating the barrier between 70 million Deaf people and social creator platforms.

01

Regulatory Accessibility

Global digital mandates require platforms to support assistive technologies. Sign Bridge provides a native sign overlay mechanism.

02

Creator Inclusivity

Over 1M Deaf Kenyans can now engage with and create viral social media trends with automatic speech-to-sign conversion.

03

Indigenous Language NLP

Extends state-of-the-art Meta AI foundation models to under-resourced East African Sign Languages: KSL (Kenya), USL (Uganda), and TSL (Tanzania).

04

Two-Way Inclusion

Deaf Creator Mode translates native KSL signs into spoken English voice and captions so hearing audiences can understand signers directly.

Architecture

Meta AI Model Stack

Each step operates via official Meta API inference endpoints.

SPEECH / AUDIO

Meta Muse Wonder 2

Robust speech transcription with Sheng dialect recognition and sub-word timestamp extraction.

LINGUISTICS

Meta Muse Spark 1.3

Structured SVO-to-SOV grammar compiler encoding KSL idioms, negations, and spatial markers.

COMPUTER VISION

Meta SAM 3.1

Real-time object and pose segmentation detecting speaker faces to compute safe quadrant overlays.

SYNTHESIS

Deterministic seed generation rendering a photorealistic Kenyan signing avatar with clear hand anatomy.

COMPOSITOR

Multi-stream video compositor with frame deduplication for accelerated rendering.

BACKEND

Stateless job management with strict file path validation and input sanitization.
